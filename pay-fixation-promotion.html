Pay fixation on promotion: how your new basic pay is set

When you are promoted to a higher level, your pay is fixed by a simple two-step rule. You also get to choose the date it is applied from, and the right choice can be worth thousands of rupees.

Last updated October 2026 · by NE Media

The rule in two steps

  1. One increment in your current level. Move one cell down your present level, which adds about 3%.
  2. Find the same amount in the new level. You are placed in the promoted level at the cell equal to that figure. If there is no exactly equal cell, you go to the next higher cell. If the new level starts higher than that figure, you start at its first cell.

This is Rule 13 of the Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016. The same method is used when you get a financial upgradation under MACP.

Example 1: Level 6 to Level 7

Basic pay ₹41,100 at Level 6.

  • Step 1: one increment in Level 6 → ₹42,300.
  • Step 2: Level 7 starts at ₹44,900, which is higher than ₹42,300, so the new basic pay is ₹44,900.
  • Gain: ₹3,800 basic pay a month, plus 60% DA on it (₹2,280) and HRA.

Example 2: Level 7 to Level 8

Basic pay ₹53,600 at Level 7.

  • Step 1: one increment in Level 7 → ₹55,200.
  • Step 2: Level 8 has a cell of exactly ₹55,200, so the new basic pay is ₹55,200.

Levels 7 and 8 overlap heavily, which is why the gain here is just one increment. Where the levels are further apart, such as Level 5 to Level 6 or Level 10 to Level 11, the jump is usually much bigger.

Your option: date of promotion or date of next increment

You can choose when the fixation is done:

  • Option A, from the date of promotion. The two steps above are applied straight away.
  • Option B, from your date of next increment (DNI) in the old level. On promotion you are placed in the new level at the cell equal to (or next above) your present pay, without the extra increment. On your next increment date in the old level, pay is fixed again: you get that annual increment and the promotion increment in the old level, and are then placed in the new level.

You must give the option in writing within one month of the promotion order. It cannot be changed later. If you do not choose, Option A applies.

Worked comparison

Level 7, basic pay ₹53,600, increment date 1 January, promoted to Level 8 on 1 October 2026.

PeriodOption A (date of promotion)Option B (DNI, 1 January)
1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026₹55,200₹53,600
1 Jan – 30 Jun 2027₹55,200₹56,900
1 Jul – 30 Sep 2027₹56,900 (next increment)₹56,900
Basic pay over the 12 months₹6,67,500₹6,72,900

How Option B reaches ₹56,900 on 1 January 2027: annual increment in Level 7 (₹53,600 → ₹55,200), then the promotion increment (₹55,200 → ₹56,900), then the matching Level 8 cell, ₹56,900. Over the first year Option B pays ₹5,400 more in basic pay, plus DA on it.

Which option should you choose?

  • Increment due soon (within about three months of promotion): Option B usually pays more, because you get both increments within a short wait.
  • Increment far away (five or six months): Option A is often better, since you get the higher pay immediately.
  • New level starts well above your pay (as in Example 1): both options often land on the first cell of the new level, so Option A, which pays it sooner, is simpler.
  • Compare the two using the cell values on the Pay Matrix page before you sign the option form, and ask your pay bill section to confirm your next increment date under each option.

Your increment date after promotion

A promotion resets your increment date, using the same rule as joining: promotion between 2 January and 1 July gives a 1 January increment date; between 2 July and 1 January gives 1 July. In the example above (promotion on 1 October) the next increment under Option A is on 1 July 2027. See the annual increment guide for details.

Promotion after MACP

  • If you already got a MACP upgradation to the same level as your promotion post, no further pay fixation is done on promotion. Only your designation changes.
  • If the promotion post is in a higher level than your MACP level, pay is fixed again by the same two steps.

Promotion and the 8th Pay Commission

If you are promoted before the 8th CPC pay is fixed, the higher basic pay is what gets multiplied by the fitment factor. If the promotion comes after the changeover date, it will be fixed under the new 8th CPC Pay Matrix once that is notified, and the arrears will reflect it. Try your new basic in the 8th Pay calculator to see what it means for your estimated 8th CPC pay and arrears.

Sources

Rule 13 of the Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 and the related clarifications of the Department of Expenditure and the Department of Personnel and Training. Special cases, such as promotion while on deputation or ad hoc promotion, follow separate orders; your office has the final word.
