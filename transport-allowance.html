Transport allowance: rates and city list

Transport Allowance (TA) helps with the cost of travelling between home and office. It is a fixed amount set by your pay level and city, plus Dearness Allowance on it.

Last updated October 2026 · by NE Media

Rates

Pay levelHigher-TA citiesOther places
Level 9 and above₹7,200₹3,600
Levels 3 to 8₹3,600₹1,800
Levels 1 and 2₹1,350₹900
  • Employees in Levels 1 and 2 with basic pay of ₹24,200 or more get the Levels 3–8 rate (₹3,600 or ₹1,800).
  • Officers in Level 14 and above who are entitled to an official car can take ₹15,750 a month instead of the car.
  • DA is added to all these amounts at the current DA rate.

Amounts with 60% DA

DA has been 60% since 1 January 2026, so TA including DA is 1.6 times the base rate:

Level 9 and above₹11,520₹5,760
Levels 3 to 8₹5,760₹2,880
Levels 1 and 2₹2,160₹1,440
Level 14+, in lieu of official car₹25,200₹25,200

Each time DA goes up, TA goes up with it. If DA rises from 60% to 63% for July 2026, TA for Level 9 and above in a higher-TA city becomes ₹11,736, an extra ₹216 a month. The DA history page lists every revision.

This list is not the same as the X, Y, Z classification used for HRA. Lucknow, Jaipur, Patna and Kochi, for example, are Y-class for HRA but get the higher TA rate. Places outside these 19 get the "other places" rate, even if they are X-class for HRA. See the HRA city list to compare.

Employees with disabilities

Employees who are blind, have an orthopaedic disability of the lower limbs, are deaf and mute, or have a spinal deformity get TA at double the normal rate, with a minimum of ₹2,250 a month, plus DA. A certificate from the appropriate medical authority is needed.

When TA is not paid

  • For a whole calendar month spent on leave, training, or tour. If you are away for only part of the month, TA is paid in full.
  • If you use an official car or Government transport between home and office.

Income tax on TA

Transport Allowance is taxable as part of salary in both tax regimes. The separate exemption for TA was replaced by the standard deduction in 2018. TA for employees with disabilities remains exempt up to a limit in the old regime.

TA and the 8th Pay Commission

When 8th CPC pay begins and DA restarts from 0%, TA including DA would drop unless the base rates are raised. The 7th CPC handled this by multiplying the old base rates by 2.25, the same factor that absorbed the 125% DA of the time: the top rate went from ₹3,200 to ₹7,200, and ₹1,600 became ₹3,600. The 8th CPC is expected to set new base rates in a similar way. Our 8th Pay calculator shows the effect on take-home pay.
