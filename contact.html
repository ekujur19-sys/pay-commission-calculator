What to write about

  • An error in the calculator: tell us the tab you used (7th Pay, 8th Pay or Arrears), your level and basic pay, and what you expected to see.
  • A rate that has changed: for example a new DA instalment or Budget tax change. A link to the official order helps.
  • Feature ideas: what would make the calculator more useful for you.
  • Commercial use: using the calculator or its content for a business needs written permission (see the Terms of Use).
  • Privacy questions or requests: see the Privacy Policy.

Please don’t send personal documents. Never email pay slips, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or passwords. We never need them, and we don’t give individual pay or tax advice. For your exact pay, contact your office or DDO.
