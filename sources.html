What is our own estimate

Some figures cannot come from an official source yet, because the 8th Pay Commission has not reported. These are clearly marked as estimates in the calculator and guides:

  • the 8th CPC fitment factor (you choose it; 1.92, 2.08, 2.28, 2.57 and 2.86 are offered as examples);
  • 8th CPC DA, HRA rates and the date new pay is actually paid;
  • future DA rises (3% every six months by default, which you can change).

How we keep figures up to date

  • We check the 8th CPC website, PIB and the Department of Expenditure for new orders.
  • When DA, allowances or tax rules change, the calculator, guides and the “Last reviewed” dates are updated.
  • Found something out of date? Email nemedia909@gmail.com, ideally with a link to the official order.
