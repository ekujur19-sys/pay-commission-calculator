Rates at a glance

House Rent Allowance

City classWhich placesHRAMinimum a month
XCities of 50 lakh people or more, with their urban areas30%₹5,400
YCities of 5 lakh to 50 lakh20%₹3,600
ZAll other places10%₹1,800

HRA is a percentage of basic pay only (not DA). These rates apply because DA is above 50%. See the HRA guide for how the rates change with DA and when HRA is not paid.

The higher transport allowance applies in 19 cities, which are not the same as the X-class cities: Lucknow, Jaipur, Patna and Kochi, for example, are Class Y for HRA but get the higher transport allowance, while Gurugram and Faridabad are Class X but get the other-places rate. No X or Y city matches. A place that is not on this list is Class Z: HRA at 10% of basic pay (minimum ₹1,800), and transport allowance at the other-places rate.

Class Z: everywhere else

Any city or town that is not in the list above is Class Z, with HRA at 10% of basic pay (minimum ₹1,800 a month) and transport allowance at the other-places rate.

Check with your office. City classes are fixed by the Ministry of Finance on the basis of the 2011 Census, and urban areas can include nearby towns. This list is compiled from public orders for guidance. Your office's pay records and the latest Government order decide your actual allowances.
