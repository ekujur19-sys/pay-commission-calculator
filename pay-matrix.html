7th Pay Commission Pay Matrix

Basic pay for every cell of every level of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix for Central Government civilian employees, plus the UGC Academic Pay Matrix for teachers.

Last updated October 2026 · by NE Media

How to read the Pay Matrix

  • Each level is a column of pay amounts. Your level depends on your post (it replaced the old pay band and Grade Pay).
  • Each cell is one step. Your annual increment (on 1 January or 1 July) moves you one cell down, which is about 3% more.
  • Cells are worked out as the previous cell × 1.03, rounded to the nearest ₹100.
  • On promotion you move to the next level, to the cell equal to or just above your pay after one notional increment.
  • DA is 60% of basic pay from 1 January 2026. The tables show basic pay with 60% DA added.
