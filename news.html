8th Pay Commission: latest news

Key developments on the 8th Central Pay Commission and related pay decisions, newest first, with a link to the official source for each.

Last updated 26 October 2026 · by NE Media

Coming up

  • Late Oct – Nov 2026ExpectedCabinet decision on DA from 1 July 2026. Still not announced as of 26 October: the Cabinet meetings of 14 and 21 October passed without it. DA stays at 60% until then. Reports expect about 3%, taking DA to around 63%, with arrears from July. See what 3% is worth at your level on the DA history page.
  • To be announcedThe Commission’s next city visit. No visit after Mumbai has been notified yet.
  • By about May 2027ExpectedThe Commission’s report is due within 18 months of its constitution on 3 November 2025.

Timeline

  1. 22–23 October 2026

    OfficialCommission visits Mumbai

    The Commission held its scheduled meetings in Mumbai with associations and unions that had requested appointments by 10 October. It follows visits to nine other places and two rounds of meetings in Delhi since May 2026. No further visit has been announced yet.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New (notice of 21 September 2026)

  2. 14 and 21 October 2026

    DAJuly 2026 DA not yet taken up by the Cabinet

    Two Cabinet meetings passed without a decision on DA and Dearness Relief from 1 July 2026. Last year the July instalment was approved on 1 October. Until it is approved, DA and DR stay at 60%; when it comes, arrears are paid from July.

    Official decisions are published by the Press Information Bureau. Background: DA hike history

  3. 7–8 October 2026

    OfficialCommission meets stakeholders in Bengaluru

    The Commission held its scheduled meetings with associations and unions in Bengaluru. Mumbai (22–23 October) followed.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  4. 21 September 2026

    OfficialCommission to visit Mumbai on 22–23 October

    The 8th CPC announced a visit to Mumbai for meetings with associations and unions, with requests due by 10 October 2026.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  5. 24 August 2026

    OfficialBengaluru visit on 7–8 October announced

    Requests for meetings during the Bengaluru visit were due by 18 September 2026.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  6. August – September 2026

    OfficialState visits: Jaipur, Chennai, Puducherry and Chandigarh

    After meetings in Delhi on 7 and 10 August, the Commission visited Jaipur (31 August – 1 September), Chennai (7–8 September), Puducherry (9 September) and Chandigarh (16–18 September). On 11 August it also partly modified its guidelines for engaging consultants.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  7. June – July 2026

    OfficialVisits to Ladakh, Lucknow, Bhubaneswar and Kolkata

    The Commission met stakeholders in Ladakh (8 June), Lucknow (22–23 June), Bhubaneswar (6–7 July) and Kolkata (9–10 July).

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  8. 29 May 2026

    OfficialDeadline for memorandum responses extended to 15 June 2026

    The Commission gave ministries, associations and other stakeholders more time to send their memoranda and responses.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  9. May 2026

    OfficialFirst interactions in Delhi

    The Commission held interactions with stakeholders in Delhi on 13–14 May 2026.

    Source: 8th CPC – What’s New

  10. 18 April 2026

    DADA raised to 60% from 1 January 2026

    The Union Cabinet approved a 2% increase in Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief, from 58% to 60% of basic pay and pension, with effect from 1 January 2026.

    Source: Cabinet release, 18 Apr 2026 · DA order, 22 Apr 2026 (PDF) · DA history

  11. 14 April 2026

    DemandStaff Side submits its memorandum

    The Staff Side of the National Council (JCM), which represents employees, asked the Commission for a fitment factor of 3.833, minimum basic pay of about ₹69,000 (now ₹18,000), 6% annual increments and HRA linked to DA. These are the employees’ demands, not decisions.

    Source: Business Today, 15 Apr 2026 · Fitment factor explained

  12. Early 2026

    OfficialQuestionnaire and memorandum submissions open

    The Commission invited views through a questionnaire and memorandum submission on its website, and later issued guidelines for engaging consultants (10 April 2026).

    Source: 8th CPC Questionnaire · Memorandum submission · Consultant circulars

  13. 3 November 2025

    Official8th Pay Commission formally constituted

    The Government notified the constitution of the Eighth Central Pay Commission in the Gazette. The Commission is based at Chanderlok Building, Janpath, New Delhi.

    Source: Gazette notification, 3 Nov 2025 (PDF)

  14. 28 October 2025

    OfficialCabinet approves terms of reference and members

    Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai was named Chairperson, Prof. Pulak Ghosh as part-time Member and Shri Pankaj Jain as Member-Secretary. The Commission is to report within 18 months, and its recommendations are normally expected to take effect from 1 January 2026.

    Source: Cabinet release, 28 Oct 2025 · 8th CPC – Terms of Reference

  15. 1 October 2025

    DADA raised to 58% from 1 July 2025

    A 3% increase in DA and DR, from 55% to 58%.

    Source: Cabinet release, 1 Oct 2025

  16. January 2025

    OfficialCabinet approves setting up the 8th Pay Commission

    The Union Cabinet approved the formation of the 8th Central Pay Commission to revise the pay and pensions of Central Government employees and pensioners.

    Source: Cabinet release, 28 Oct 2025 (records the January 2025 announcement)

What has not been decided yet

The fitment factor, the new Pay Matrix, allowances, pension revision and the date new pay is actually paid are all still open. Any figures you see reported for these are expectations, not decisions.
