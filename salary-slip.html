Your pay slip explained, line by line

What each earning and deduction on a Central Government pay slip means, how it is worked out, and how to check that your salary is right.

Last updated October 2026 · by NE Media

A worked example

Level 7, basic pay ₹47,600, posted in Delhi, on NPS, DA 60%, new tax regime.

EarningsAmountHow it is worked out
Basic pay₹47,600Your cell in the Pay Matrix
Dearness Allowance (DA)₹28,56060% of basic pay
House Rent Allowance (HRA)₹14,28030% of basic pay (X-class city)
Transport Allowance (TA) with DA₹5,760₹3,600 + 60% DA (higher-TA city)
Gross pay₹96,200
DeductionsAmountHow it is worked out
NPS (employee share)₹7,61610% of basic pay + DA
CGHS₹650Fixed by pay level (Levels 7–11)
CGEGIS₹60Fixed by group (Group B)
Income tax (TDS)₹0Yearly taxable income below ₹12 lakh after the standard deduction, so no tax in the new regime
Total deductions₹8,326

Net pay: ₹87,874. The Government also puts ₹10,662 (14%) into your NPS account. It does not appear in gross pay, but it is part of what you earn. Check your own figures with the salary calculator.

Earnings

Basic pay

The amount in your cell of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. It changes with your annual increment, promotion or MACP. All the percentage-based allowances are worked out on it. See every cell on the Pay Matrix page.

Dearness Allowance (DA)

A percentage of basic pay, revised twice a year to offset inflation. It is 60% from 1 January 2026; the decision for July 2026 is still awaited. See the DA history.

House Rent Allowance (HRA)

30%, 20% or 10% of basic pay for X, Y and Z class cities, with minimums of ₹5,400, ₹3,600 and ₹1,800. Not paid if you live in Government accommodation. See the HRA city list.

Transport Allowance (TA)

A fixed amount by pay level and city, plus DA on it. See transport allowance rates.

Other earnings you may see

  • Non-Practising Allowance (NPA) for doctors: 20% of basic pay; DA is paid on it too.
  • Children Education Allowance: paid once a year on a claim, not monthly. See the CEA guide.
  • Special allowances for hard areas, the North East, Ladakh, risk or particular duties.
  • Overtime, night duty or honorarium, where applicable.
  • Arrears: back pay for a DA increase, an increment or a promotion, often shown as a separate line.

Deductions

NPS or GPF

  • NPS (joined on or after 1 January 2004): 10% of basic pay + DA. Employees who chose UPS also contribute 10%. See NPS tax benefits.
  • GPF (joined before 2004, old pension scheme): at least 6% of basic pay, and you can choose more, up to your basic pay. GPF earns interest set every quarter by the Government.

CGHS

Central Government Health Scheme contribution, by pay level:

Pay levelPer month
Levels 1 to 5₹250
Level 6₹450
Levels 7 to 11₹650
Level 12 and above₹1,000

It is deducted if you live in a CGHS city, whether or not you use CGHS. In cities without CGHS, you are covered by the Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules instead, and nothing is deducted.

CGEGIS (group insurance)

Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme: ₹120 a month for Group A (Level 10 and above), ₹60 for Group B (Levels 6–9) and ₹30 for Group C (Levels 1–5). Part goes to an insurance fund, which pays a fixed amount to your family if you die in service, and part to a savings fund paid back with interest when you leave service.

Income tax (TDS)

Your office estimates your tax for the whole year and deducts it in equal monthly instalments. Tell your office which regime you choose at the start of the year. If your tax changes (for example, due to arrears), the rest of the year's deductions are adjusted. The yearly certificate of tax deducted (earlier Form 16) is given after the year ends. See old vs new tax regime.

Licence fee

If you live in Government accommodation, a licence fee is deducted, depending on the type of quarters, and no HRA is paid.

Other deductions

  • Professional tax in states that levy it (for example Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal); not in Delhi.
  • Recovery of advances: House Building Advance, festival advance or any overpayment.
  • Cooperative society, welfare fund, association or union subscriptions, where you have authorised them.

How to check your pay slip

  1. Confirm your level and cell match your last pay fixation order.
  2. Check DA is at the current rate and HRA matches your city's class.
  3. Check NPS is exactly 10% of basic + DA, and that the 14% Government share reaches your PRAN account (visible online).
  4. Check the yearly tax estimate against the calculator, especially in the month after a DA increase.
  5. Raise any mistake with your Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO) promptly; arrears are paid when it is corrected.

Sources
