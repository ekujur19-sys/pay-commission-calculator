1. Who runs this website

Pay Commission Calculator (“the website”) is a free online tool that estimates salary, allowances and income tax for employees under the 7th and projected 8th Central Pay Commission. It is created and run by NE Media, the name under which an individual based in India operates this website (referred to in this policy as “NE Media”, “I”, “me” or “my”). NE Media is not an incorporated company.

For the limited personal data described below, I am the “Data Fiduciary” as defined in India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

This website is independent and is not connected to, endorsed by or run on behalf of the Government of India, any State Government, any Ministry or any Pay Commission.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit or use the website 8thpay.in, including the calculator, its pages and any ads shown on it. It does not cover websites you reach through links or advertisements; those sites have their own policies.

By using the website, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

3. No personal details are asked for

This website does not ask you to provide any personal details, at any point. In particular:

  • There is no registration, account, login or password.
  • There are no contact forms, feedback forms, surveys, comment boxes or newsletter sign-ups.
  • There are no payments, so no card, UPI or bank details are ever requested.
  • The website never asks for your name, phone number, email address, postal address, date of birth, gender, photograph, employee ID, department, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, passwords or health information.

Figures you enter in the calculator

To give you an estimate, the calculator lets you enter or select anonymous figures such as pay level, basic pay, city or HRA class, DA rate, pension contribution, allowances, deductions, rent paid, investments and tax regime. None of these identify you.

  • Processed on your device only. All calculations run in your web browser using code on the page. These figures are not sent to me or to anyone else.
  • Not stored. Your entries are not saved in any database, cookie or browser storage. They disappear when you reload or close the page.
  • Share links. If you use “Share this result” or “Copy link”, the figures you entered are written into the web address so the calculator can open with them. That link goes only where you choose to send it, for example a WhatsApp chat. Anyone who has the link can see those figures, so share it only with people you trust.
  • Result postcard. When you share on WhatsApp from the calculator, a postcard image showing your result is drawn by your own browser. It goes only to the chat or app you choose, so share it only with people you are happy to show those figures to.
  • Download PDF. The pay slip or arrears statement is created by your own browser from the figures on screen. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Share buttons. The WhatsApp, Telegram and Facebook buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to those services unless you click a button, and what happens next is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Please keep it anonymous. Do not type personal details into any field, including the free-text names for custom allowances and deductions.

Because I never receive these figures, I cannot view, correct, export or delete them. You stay in full control of them.

If you choose to email me, I will receive your email address and whatever you write. That is the only way I would ever receive information you give directly, and it is used only to reply to you.
