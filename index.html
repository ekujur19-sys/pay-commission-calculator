How this is worked out. The city search uses the 7th CPC X and Y city lists; Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and Faridabad are treated as Delhi, and any city not listed is Class Z. HRA is 30/20/10% of basic when DA is 50% or more, 27/18/9% between 25% and 50%, and 24/16/8% below 25%, with minimums of ₹5,400/₹3,600/₹1,800. Transport Allowance uses the 7th CPC slabs (X city treated as a higher-TA city) plus DA on it. 8th CPC figures are estimates: new basic = 7th CPC basic × fitment factor, rounded to ₹100. 8th CPC HRA uses the rates you choose in the HRA section (today's 30/20/10% by default), and transport allowance follows the 7th CPC rules. Allowances and deductions you add use the same monthly amount in both pay commissions; suggested amounts (CGHS and CGEGIS by level, Children Education Allowance for one child, professional tax) can be edited. Income tax is worked out for a full year at this monthly pay, using the new and old regime slabs from the Union Budget 2025.
