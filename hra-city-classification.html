HRA rates and the X, Y, Z city list

How much House Rent Allowance Central Government employees get, how the city you work in decides the rate, and how HRA changes as DA rises.

Last updated October 2026 · by NE Media

Find your city. Search any city to see its HRA class (X, Y or Z), whether it gets the higher transport allowance, and every rate that applies: HRA and TA by city.

Current HRA rates

House Rent Allowance (HRA) is a percentage of your basic pay (not basic plus DA). The percentage depends on the class of the city where you are posted. Since DA crossed 50% on 1 January 2024, the rates are:

City classWho it coversHRAMinimum per month
XCities with a population of 50 lakh or more30%₹5,400
YCities with a population of 5 to 50 lakh20%₹3,600
ZAll other places10%₹1,800

Example: Level 6, basic pay ₹35,400, posted in a Y class city: HRA = 20% × ₹35,400 = ₹7,080 a month.

How HRA rates change with DA

The 7th Pay Commission set HRA to rise automatically as DA rises:

When DA isXYZ
Below 25% (from 1 January 2016)24%16%8%
25% or more (applied from July 2021)27%18%9%
50% or more (from 1 January 2024)30%20%10%

At the 8th Pay Commission, DA resets to 0%. The 8th CPC will set its own HRA rules, which are not known yet. If it follows the 7th CPC pattern, HRA could restart at 24/16/8%. The calculator lets you choose between today’s rates and the reset rates.

Z class

Every place not in the X or Y lists is Z class, with HRA at 10% of basic pay and a minimum of ₹1,800 a month.

Check your office’s classification. City classes are fixed by the Ministry of Finance, Department of Expenditure, and are revised from time to time. This list is compiled from public sources for guidance and may be incomplete. Your office’s pay records and the latest Government order decide your actual HRA.

HRA and income tax

Under the old tax regime, part of your HRA can be tax-free if you pay rent. The exempt amount is the lowest of:

  • the HRA you receive;
  • rent paid minus 10% of basic pay plus DA;
  • 50% of basic pay plus DA in the eight listed cities, or 40% elsewhere.

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Rules, 2026 extended the 50% limit from four cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) to eight, adding Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. You must also give your landlord's PAN if the rent is over ₹1 lakh a year, and disclose if the landlord is a relative. Under the new tax regime, HRA is fully taxable. The calculator’s tax section works this out for you.

Example (old regime): Level 7, basic pay ₹47,600, DA 60%, in Pune, paying rent of ₹20,000 a month. Basic plus DA is ₹9,13,920 a year and HRA is ₹1,71,360 (30%). Rent minus 10% of basic plus DA is ₹2,40,000 − ₹91,392 = ₹1,48,608. 50% of basic plus DA is ₹4,56,960. The lowest, ₹1,48,608, is tax-free; the remaining ₹22,752 of HRA is taxable.

When HRA is not paid, or is paid differently

  • Government accommodation: no HRA is paid while you live in Government quarters, and a licence fee is deducted instead. This also applies if your spouse has been allotted Government accommodation at the same station and you share it.
  • Sharing with parents or relatives: if your parent, child or spouse has been allotted Government accommodation and you live with them, you do not get HRA.
  • On transfer: HRA is based on your new place of posting. If your family stays back at the old station, you can keep drawing HRA at the old station's rate for a limited period in certain cases, such as the end of the school year.
  • On leave, tour or training: HRA continues at the rate of your place of posting.
  • Hard and North-East areas: special HRA orders apply in some remote and border areas.

How cities are classified

The classes are based on the population of the urban agglomeration in the latest census (the 2011 Census for the current list), not on the size of the municipal area alone. A city can be upgraded when a new census shows its population has crossed 50 lakh (to X) or 5 lakh (to Y). The 2027 Census may lead to a revised list, possibly around the same time as the 8th CPC.

The HRA classes are not the same as the list of cities that get the higher transport allowance, or the list for the 50% income tax limit above.
